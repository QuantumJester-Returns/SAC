# Milestone 1 — Arena Specification

## Purpose

Create the first convincing, explorable environment for Sea Among The Clouds. The arena must communicate scale, atmosphere, and a distinct visual identity before the project expands into full gameplay.

## Player experience target

The player should feel surrounded by an immense open world: a broad reflective sea, a sky that feels distant and deep, and cloud formations that occupy unusual positions beneath and around the sea's altitude.

## Required visual pillars

### 1. Sea
- Deep blue overall appearance, with readable variation from lighting and viewing angle.
- Broad, calm surface with restrained, subtle ripples.
- Reflections that respond coherently to the sky, sun, and cloud environment.
- No distracting high-frequency noise or exaggerated waves in the initial look.

### 2. Sky and atmosphere
- Strong sense of vertical and horizontal scale.
- Atmospheric depth and a clear sun direction.
- A coherent horizon treatment; avoid an obvious hard edge where the world ends.
- Sky and water lighting must feel like parts of the same environment.

### 3. Clouds
- Large-scale cloud forms at and below the sea's apparent altitude.
- Clear depth separation between near, middle-distance, and far formations.
- Natural variation in shape, density, and lighting.
- Cloud placement should reinforce the central surreal premise, not look like a conventional skybox pasted onto the scene.

### 4. Lighting
- A deliberate key sunlight direction.
- Consistent lighting across clouds, water, and atmospheric scattering.
- Reflections and highlights should support readability rather than overwhelm the scene.

### 5. Scale and exploration
- The initial arena should feel spacious and open.
- Establish a stable player viewpoint and basic movement test area.
- Use distant environmental landmarks or cloud layers to communicate scale without clutter.

## Functional acceptance criteria

- Project opens in the selected Unreal Engine 5 version.
- A dedicated arena map can be loaded and viewed.
- The player can enter the arena and move around using a basic test pawn.
- Water, sky, clouds, and sunlight render together without obvious visual or performance failures.
- The scene can be captured from repeatable camera positions for visual comparisons.

## Explicit non-goals

This milestone does not include:
- Multiplayer, matchmaking, or networking.
- Lobby systems or menus beyond what is needed to launch the test map.
- Dungeons, missions, or world progression.
- Crafting, inventory, equipment, or ranking systems.
- Finished combat, abilities, or progression balancing.

## Completion definition

Milestone 1 is complete when the arena is stable, explorable, visually coherent, and provides a dependable foundation for later gameplay prototyping. A beautiful static screenshot alone is not sufficient; the environment must also work as a real-time playable space.
